ACB Medication Reviewer

Paste a medication list and review anticholinergic burden.

This static tool recognises many common UK generic and brand names, gives a per-drug ACB score where mapped, totals the score, flags selected high cognitive-risk medicine classes, and shows STOPP-relevant review prompts where a medicine name is contextually relevant.

Review required:Any output from this website must be reviewed by the user. Matching, burden scores, and suggestions do not replace clinical judgement, indication review, dose review, duration review, renal review, or local policy.
Scoring basis
ACB scale dataset with cited references.
STOPP layer
Contextual prompts only, not automatic breach detection.
Hosting
Static HTML + JSON, suitable for GitHub Pages with .nojekyll.

Paste one item per line, or a comma-separated list. Brand names and common misspellings are partially supported.
