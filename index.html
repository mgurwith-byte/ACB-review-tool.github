ACB & AEC Medication Reviewer

Paste a medication list and review anticholinergic burden.

This static tool maps UK generic and brand names to the published ACB (Anticholinergic Cognitive Burden, Boustani 2008/2012) and AEC (Anticholinergic Effect on Cognition, Bishara 2017) scales, flags high cognitive-risk medicine classes, and shows STOPP-relevant review prompts where a medicine name is contextually relevant.

Review required:Any output from this website must be reviewed by the user. Matching, burden scores, and suggestions do not replace clinical judgement, indication review, dose review, duration review, renal review, or local policy. Absence of a drug from these scales does not imply it is safe or non-anticholinergic.

References used in the site

  • Anticholinergic Cognitive Burden Scale 2012 PDF (Indiana University) — primary source for ACB scores.
  • NHS Somerset ACB 2012 PDF.
  • Bishara D et al. Anticholinergic effect on cognition (AEC). Int J Geriatr Psychiatry 2017;32(6):650-656. — primary source for AEC scores.

  • ACB Calculator (acbcalc.com) — useful external cross-check across multiple anticholinergic scales (Boustani, Bishara, Duran, ADS). Not the source for this site's scores.
